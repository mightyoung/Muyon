## Required local behavior

1. Initialize only via explicit trusted synthetic fixture initialization, never by read/lookup/commit of a missing or incompatible database. Preserve existing database identity and validate the provisional lab format; no migrations, source substitution or backup restore. One physical database contains supplier rows, intent bindings, commit history and receipts.
2. Rust validates complete raw UTF-16 using unchanged RD-P01: raw limit 2000 units, XML before trim/NFC, nonempty and at most 200 codepoints. Only supplier name may change. Preserve other rows and fields. M04/M05 Unicode corpus limitations remain; do not claim global Unicode equivalence.
3. Bind stable intent to full synthetic domain, supplier identity, operation, exact raw arguments, original expected revision and plan/candidate binding. Epoch/request transport identity cannot create another intent. Same intent with altered binding conflicts without business writes. Exact committed replay returns the original receipt before stale CAS is reapplied.
4. Persist the bound pending intent before attempting a business effect. Claim ownership atomically; a competing process must not dispatch an already-pending intent. Pending without a verified committed receipt remains unknown, including after crash, busy/error or lookup notFound; recovery/lookup never automatically retries it. A proven validation/CAS rejection may retain its bound rejection but is not a business receipt. This is not a new product status contract.
5. The applied business transaction atomically performs the guarded supplier name/revision update, synthetic commit history, committed idempotency fact and actual original receipt. All four are committed together. CAS checks current physical revision inside the transaction. Distinct intents racing at the same revision can produce at most one applied update; exact duplicates cannot produce a second update/event/receipt. Trusted new same-normalized-name intent still increments revision once and gets a separate receipt.
6. Use parameter-bound SQL and checked SQLite result/affected-row codes; no user SQL execution. Preserve canonical revision values without SQLite signed-integer or floating conversion loss, including values above 2^53 and the u64 overflow boundary from RD-P01. A synthetic commit log may record a same-name accepted update; distinguish it from existing product field-change log semantics.
7. Receipt is generated from actual database effect data, bound to its original intent/arguments/domain/source, and independently verifiable against committed local history. It cannot be manufactured from a client's success flag or a missing result. Current supplier revision/name and historical receipt remain separate after later commits.
8. Read/preview/lookup do not write business state, intent rows, receipt/history or create/migrate databases. State explicit SQLite read-only open flags and any necessary engine journal recovery assumptions; do not claim byte/mtime invariance of SQLite bookkeeping unless physically verified. Missing/corrupt/incompatible data fails closed. A read-only failure requiring SQLite recovery is unavailable/unknown, not permission to repair or dispatch.

## Actual verification

First collect behavior assertion RED from compilable executables in real temporary directories. Use separate OS processes for requests, process death and reopen; independent Python stdlib SQLite queries observe physical rows, committed/rejected/pending facts, commit log and receipts. Count unique test methods and fault subcases separately. Build/import errors do not count as RED.

Required faults: pending persisted before business transaction; after actual SQL business update; after history insert; after committed intent status; after receipt insert but before COMMIT; after COMMIT before reply. Include actual SIGKILL with a deterministic barrier and an actual receipt-insertion failure such as a private fixture trigger. After precommit failure/reopen, no business/log/committed-intent/receipt half-state is allowed. After lost response following COMMIT, all committed facts survive and duplicate replay produces zero new effect. Preserve the independent pending boundary rather than interpreting it as success or zero dispatch.

Exercise concurrent equal-intent requests and distinct-intent same-CAS races with real processes. Independently read physical counts and original bindings. Required inverse mutations remove/break the shared transaction, CAS guard, duplicate guard, binding comparison and unknown-no-dispatch rule; each must compile successfully and fail an intended behavior assertion. Restore source hashes and rerun the complete new lab suite. Keep raw logs outside Git.
